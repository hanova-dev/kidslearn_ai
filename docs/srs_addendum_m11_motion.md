# SRS Addendum (draft) — Module M11: Motion Comic Engine

*Draft for KidsLearn AI SRS v1.2. Status: proposed by the team on 14 Nov 2026; not yet merged into the SRS. Phase 1 is built (decision 033).*

## 3.11 Module M11 — Motion Comic Engine

*Description: Each story page plays like a short animated scene: a slow camera move over the FLUX illustration, the hero moving on its own layer, gentle ambient effects, and timing synced to the narration. Everything is rendered on the device from the existing still image and a small motion directive written by the story model; no video is generated by AI, no network call happens during playback, and nothing new is sent to any third-party service.*

Priority: Should-Have. Primary Actor: Child (playback), System (directives and preparation).

| **ID** | **Requirement Description** | **Priority** |
|---|---|---|
| FR-MOTION-01 | The system shall obtain one motion directive per scene from the story model in the same request as the story text, using a closed vocabulary for camera move, focus point, character action, ambient effect and mood, plus an optional cue word from the scene text. | M |
| FR-MOTION-02 | The system shall validate every motion directive against the closed vocabulary, replace missing or invalid values with defaults derived from the mood, accept the cue word only if it occurs in the scene text, and never fail a story because of motion output. | M |
| FR-MOTION-03 | The system shall store each scene's motion directive with the scene in local SQLite (`scenes.motion_json`) and shall play scenes created before M11 with calm default motion. Directives shall sync with the scene text once the SyncEngine (M9) exists; layer image paths remain local, like `image_path`. | M |
| FR-MOTION-04 | The system shall compute, with the team-built SceneChoreographer, a deterministic keyframe timeline for camera transform, character-layer transform, effect start/stop and mascot mouth state from the directive, the narration timeline, the active AgeExperiencePolicy's MotionProfile and the text direction. | M |
| FR-MOTION-05 | The system shall animate only the picture area; story text and the word being read shall never move. In Text Mode, camera motion shall settle before the microphone is active and stay still while the child reads and is scored. | M |
| FR-MOTION-06 | When the device setting "Remove animations" is on, the system shall show the still scene with no motion. | M |
| FR-MOTION-07 | The system shall ease the camera to the directive's focus point when the cue word is spoken, using the TTS engine's word-progress callbacks where available and a word-length approximation otherwise (FR-TTS-04). | S |
| FR-MOTION-08 | While the mascot narrates, the system shall switch its mouth between open and closed on word boundaries. | S |
| FR-MOTION-09 | The system shall show ambient effects from the vocabulary, rendered on the device; a missing effect asset or renderer shall show no effect and never crash. | S |
| FR-MOTION-10 | The system shall mirror every directional move (pans, slide-ins) when the story is shown right-to-left (Urdu). | M |
| FR-MOTION-11 | The system shall cut the hero into its own layer once, after a scene image is saved, through a SubjectSegmentationService with an on-device primary adapter and a single-layer fallback; character offsets shall stay within about 2–3% of the picture width; placeholder images still receive camera motion. | C |
| FR-MOTION-12 | If frames drop on a low-end device, the system shall turn off ambient effects first and then the character layer, keeping camera motion. | S |

## 5.5 Third-party service selection — new rows

| **Capability** | **Primary** | **Fallback** | **Notes** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motion directives | Same Groq `openai/gpt-oss-120b` story request (one line per page) | Calm default motion | No extra call. Fits the free 8,000 tokens per minute: about 1,700 prompt + 5,000 answer limit, `reasoning_effort: low` (decision 033). |
| Motion playback | On-device Flutter animation (`AnimationController`, `Transform`) | Still image | No service, no network, no data leaves the phone. |
| Ambient effects | Drawn on device with `CustomPainter` (proposed) | No effect | No package and no asset licences needed; `lottie` (MIT) with licensed assets was considered. |
| Subject segmentation | Google ML Kit Subject Segmentation (candidate only) | Single layer (no segmentation) | Not adopted yet: the API is beta; the model is downloaded through Google Play services; ML Kit sends usage metrics to Google (images stay on the device); its terms do not address children's apps. To be decided after a test on our FLUX images (NFR-PRIV-06). |

## Non-functional requirement updates (proposed)

- **NFR-PERF-05** — Motion playback shall target 60 fps on a 3 GB Android 8.0+ device and degrade gracefully (FR-MOTION-12).
- **NFR-USE-06** — Child-facing motion shall be slow and predictable: nothing flashes more than 3 times per second, no screen shake, no fast zooms (SRS 10).
- **NFR-PRIV-07** — M11 shall send no new data to any third-party service; motion directives are produced in the existing story request and contain no child data (NFR-PRIV-05).
- **NFR-REL-06** — Invalid or missing motion output shall never fail story generation (FR-MOTION-02).
